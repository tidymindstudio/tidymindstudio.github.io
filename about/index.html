About TidyMind Studio

These printables are for adults with ADHD or executive dysfunction, students who keep losing the thread between "I should study" and actually opening the book, and anyone who is currently too overwhelmed to use a normal planner. You don't need a diagnosis to use them. If regular productivity systems make you feel worse — too many boxes, too much setup, a layout that only works if you never miss a day — you're the person these were made for.

Everything in the shop is a printable PDF. The pages are undated, so you can start on a Wednesday in October and not waste anything. They're designed to be low-ink: mostly white space, no heavy colour blocks, no decorative borders eating your toner. Sized for US Letter, so they print from a home printer or a library printer without scaling. You get the download link immediately after checkout on Payhip, and you can print any page as many times as you want for your own use.

One plain note: nothing here is medical advice. These are paper tools, not treatment. They can make a bad week slightly more manageable, but they can't diagnose you, they can't replace medication or therapy, and they won't fix the underlying thing. If you're struggling, a doctor or therapist is the right person to talk to.
